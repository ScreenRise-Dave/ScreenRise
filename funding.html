Support ScreenRise

Every ScreenRise production creates opportunities for emerging actors, writers, directors and crew to gain valuable industry experience, develop their skills and build recognised credits.

As a Community Interest Company, our focus is not profit. Our focus is creating opportunities for people who are seeking their first steps into the screen industries.

Why Support Matters

Producing high-quality short films requires equipment, locations, production resources, insurance, post-production facilities and professional support.

Every contribution helps us create more opportunities for emerging talent to participate in meaningful film productions and develop the experience needed to progress their careers.

Ways To Support ScreenRise

  • Financial Donations – Help fund future productions and participant opportunities.
  • Sponsorship – Support specific projects, productions or talent development initiatives.
  • Partnerships – Collaborate with ScreenRise to create employability and learning opportunities.
  • Facilities & Resources – Provide locations, equipment, expertise or production support.
  • Commissioned Productions – Engage ScreenRise to produce film content while helping to create opportunities for emerging talent.

The Impact Of Your Support

Your support helps participants gain practical experience, develop professional confidence, build portfolios and secure recognised production credits.

Together we can make the screen industries more accessible and create pathways that help talented people turn ambition into opportunity.

If you would like to support ScreenRise or explore sponsorship and partnership opportunities, please get in touch.
