Creating opportunities for emerging filmmakers through real productions and real experience.

ScreenRise produces ambitious short films that give emerging actors, writers, directors and crew the opportunity to gain real production experience and recognised industry credits.

Whether you're stepping onto your first set or building your portfolio, ScreenRise helps talented people develop their skills through genuine film productions with real audiences and real outcomes.

What You Gain

  • Verified IMDb credits
  • Hands-on production experience
  • Festival and showreel material
  • Collaboration with other emerging creatives
  • Mentoring and practical industry insight

We believe talent should have the opportunity to be seen, developed and celebrated.

Your first credit could be the start of something bigger.
