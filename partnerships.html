Partnerships

ScreenRise works with universities, colleges, schools, community groups and creative organisations to create meaningful pathways into the screen industries.

Our productions provide emerging talent with practical experience, recognised credits and professional development opportunities through participation in real film projects.

Learning Through Real Productions

We believe that many of the most valuable skills in film and television are developed through practical experience.

ScreenRise productions create opportunities for participants to develop their skills within authentic production environments rather than simulated classroom exercises.

Participants can gain experience across a wide range of disciplines including acting, directing, writing, camera, sound, editing, production and marketing.

Benefits For Educational Partners

  • Real-world industry experience for students
  • Portfolio and showreel development
  • Verified production credits
  • Cross-disciplinary collaboration opportunities
  • Enhanced employability outcomes
  • Access to practical project-based learning

Supporting Teaching And Assessment

Our partnership model is designed to complement existing educational programmes rather than replace them.

Students are able to apply classroom learning within live productions and gain valuable evidence of their skills and achievements.

ScreenRise's intellectual property framework is designed to support the appropriate use of project outputs for portfolios, academic assessment, presentations and showcase activities.

A Shared Mission

ScreenRise is committed to improving access to careers in the screen industries. By working together, educational institutions, community organisations and ScreenRise can create opportunities that help emerging talent take meaningful first steps towards professional careers.

If your organisation shares this mission, we would love to explore partnership opportunities.
