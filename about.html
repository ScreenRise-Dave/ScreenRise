About ScreenRise

ScreenRise is a Community Interest Company dedicated to improving access to careers in the screen industries.

Many talented people struggle to secure their first opportunity because they lack the experience, credits and professional networks that employers and productions often expect.

We exist to help remove those barriers.

Our Mission

Our purpose is to create opportunities for people who are seeking their first steps into the film and television industries.

We achieve this by producing high-quality film projects that provide:

  • Practical industry experience
  • Skills development and mentoring
  • Portfolio and showreel material
  • Recognised production credits
  • Professional networks and connections

Why We Do It

We believe that opportunity should not be limited by background, connections or previous experience.

Film production is our mechanism. Creating opportunity is our purpose.

As a Community Interest Company, any surplus generated is reinvested into future productions, participant development and community benefit.
