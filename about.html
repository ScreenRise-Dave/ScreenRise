Our Purpose

ScreenRise exists to give new filmmakers their essential first step in the screen industry.

We create meaningful opportunities by producing short films that:

  • Provide verified IMDb‑listed credits
  • Offer authentic on‑set production experience
  • Support emerging talent through mentorship and guidance

Our mission is accessibility, opportunity, and high‑quality storytelling for all new voices.
